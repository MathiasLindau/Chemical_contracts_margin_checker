# Chemical Supply Agreement CON-2023-0002

Between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (Supplier) and **Chemical Solutions Co.** (Buyer). This is a term contract, not a spot confirmation.

## Product specification
The product is Chloroform. Monthly nominations shall not be less than 20.0 metric tons and shall not exceed 80.0 metric tons.

## Price and indexation
The firm base price is 900.0 USD per metric ton. An energy adder of 7.0% and a feedstock adder of 3.0% apply to that base. Adders are calculated monthly from the Supplier's published formula.

## Payment
Invoices are due 45 days after the invoice date. Title passes on payment in full; risk passes on delivery.

## Logistics and quality window
Transit shall not exceed 14 days. Remaining shelf life on arrival must be at least 180 days. 3 laytime days are included; thereafter demurrage accrues at the terminal tariff.

## Liquidated damages
Failure to take or supply the minimum monthly quantity is liquidated at **2000.0 USD**, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss.

## Force majeure
Force Majeure includes strikes and severe weather.

## Law
English law. Exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Arbitration is not required unless both parties later agree in writing.
