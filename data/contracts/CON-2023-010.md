# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-010)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Nu Chemical Partners**, a Nevada entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Ethanol
- **Base Price:** 520.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 75.0 tons up to a maximum of 300.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Contract ('Contract') is made and entered into by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Nu Chemical Partners ('Purchaser'). The Supplier agrees to deliver Ethanol under the agreed terms in Exhibit A. A right of inspection is provided to the Purchaser upon delivery. The Supplier guarantees that the Product conforms to the specifications outlined herein. Should Force Majeure delays occur, the Supplier will provide notice as promptly as possible, detailing expected durations and implications. This Contract is governed by the laws of the State of Nevada, and all disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **6000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
General failures of logistics causing interruptions in service will qualify.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Total liability not exceeding the amount of money received for the order that is subject to dispute.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
