Take-or-pay minimum 120.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 160.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

## Credit
Payment within 90 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

Maximum transit 28 days. Minimum remaining life 30 days. Free time 7 days.

## Default money
Liquidated damages of **40000.0 GBP** apply to an uncured volume shortfall.

## Excused non-performance

## Dispute board
