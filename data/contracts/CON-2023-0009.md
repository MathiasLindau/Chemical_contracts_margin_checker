# International Sale Contract — CON-2023-0009

Seller: CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC. Buyer: **Synthetic Fibers Corp.**. Seat of arbitration: Singapore.

## Goods
Nylon 66, commercial grade, unless a tighter spec is attached as Schedule 1.

## Quantity band
Take-or-pay minimum 30.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 120.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

## Commercial terms
Unit price 1350.0 USD/MT. Energy cost recovery 5.0%. Raw-material recovery 15.0%. Settlement currency is the contract currency.

## Credit
Payment within 30 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

## Delivery discipline
Maximum transit 7 days. Minimum remaining life 180 days. Free time 15 days.

## Default money
Liquidated damages of **2000.0 USD** apply to an uncured volume shortfall.

## Excused non-performance
Temporary ban on imports.

## Dispute board
SIAC Rules, one arbitrator, English language. Singapore law governs.
