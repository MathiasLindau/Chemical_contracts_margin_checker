## Goods

Take-or-pay minimum 150.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 400.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

Payment within 30 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

## Delivery discipline

## Default money

## Excused non-performance

## Dispute board
