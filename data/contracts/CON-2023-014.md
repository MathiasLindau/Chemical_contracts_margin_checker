# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-014)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Rho Manufacturing Group**, a Massachusetts entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Urea
- **Base Price:** 360.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 40.0 tons up to a maximum of 200.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Contract ('Contract') is entered by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and Rho Manufacturing Group for Urea. Both parties understand the importance of regulatory compliance and terms for quality assurance. The minimum order of 40 tons will govern transactions with special terms noted in Appendix B. It is established that under no circumstance shall either party be liable for indirect damages. The parties acknowledge governing law as Massachusetts, agreeing to settle disputes primarily via arbitration.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **4200.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
International trade embargoes represent valid force majeure events.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability is confined to the contract sums arising from the payment of the specific order tied to the breach.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
