# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-006)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Zeta Chemical Holdings**, a Ohio entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Benzene
- **Base Price:** 950.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 50.0 tons up to a maximum of 200.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Distribution Agreement ('Agreement') is made and entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Zeta Chemical Holdings ('Client'). The Supplier will produce and deliver Benzene in compliance with regulatory standards in accordance with the documentation provided in Appendix A. The transaction will follow FOB delivery terms, transferring the risk upon delivery acceptance at the designated location. The Supplier guarantees Product quality will meet the technical specifications as detailed herein. In the event of any loss due to Force Majeure events, neither party will be held liable. For any disputes arising within this Agreement, resolution shall be sought in accordance with Ohio state law, wherein each party agrees to submit to the jurisdiction of the competent courts.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **8000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Severe weather impacting logistical operations will apply.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Capped at the total value paid under this Agreement for the year in which the claim arose.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
