# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-008)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Lambda Tech Solutions**, a Virginia entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Sodium Carbonate
- **Base Price:** 290.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 40.0 tons up to a maximum of 180.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Contract ('Contract') is made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Lambda Tech Solutions ('Buyer'). The Supplier is responsible for providing Sodium Carbonate subject to a minimum purchase described above. The rules of delivery specify that the Supplier will ship the Product FOB to the specified location. The Buyer accepts risk of loss once the Product is delivered. The Supplier guarantees that all products supplied comply with all applicable regulatory standards. In the event of controversy arising from this Contract, the parties will seek resolution under Virginia law and agree to exploration via non-binding arbitration if necessary.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **3500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Concerned disruptions due to legal and governmental orders will be manageable under the force majeure clause.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability capped at the amount paid under the agreement for claims in the preceding three months.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
