# Chemical Supply Agreement CON-2023-0062

Between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (Supplier) and **Lisbon Cork Chemicals** (Buyer). This is a term contract, not a spot confirmation.

## Product specification
The product is Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene. Monthly nominations shall not be less than 40.0 metric tons and shall not exceed 50.0 metric tons.

## Price and indexation
The firm base price is 670.0 EUR per metric ton. An energy adder of 2.5% and a feedstock adder of 0.0% apply to that base. Adders are calculated monthly from the Supplier's published formula.

## Payment
Invoices are due 0 days after the invoice date. Title passes on payment in full; risk passes on delivery.

## Logistics and quality window
Transit shall not exceed 21 days. Remaining shelf life on arrival must be at least 270 days. 5 laytime days are included; thereafter demurrage accrues at the terminal tariff.

## Liquidated damages
Failure to take or supply the minimum monthly quantity is liquidated at **1200.0 EUR**, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss.

## Force majeure
Grid failure at the producing plant lasting over 48 hours, documented by the local TSO.

## Law
English law. Exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Arbitration is not required unless both parties later agree in writing.
