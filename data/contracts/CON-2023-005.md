# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-005)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Epsilon Enterprises**, a Illinois entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Sodium Hydroxide
- **Base Price:** 370.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 75.0 tons up to a maximum of 300.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Sales Agreement ('Agreement') is entered into by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (the 'Seller') and Epsilon Enterprises (the 'Buyer'). The Seller hereby agrees to sell, and the Buyer agrees to purchase, Sodium Hydroxide (the 'Product') under the terms outlined in this Agreement. Quantities will adhere to the provided limits, and the Buyer shall provide written forecasts by the 20th of each month. The Seller warrants quality compliance per ANSI standards. Delivery terms will be shipped FOB Buyer’s facility. The Buyer shall inspect and provide a notice of non-conformity within 10 business days of delivery. Liability for damages caused by the Product is limited pursuant to Section 10, and 'Force Majeure' shall be explicitly defined as indicated in Section 15 herein.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **4000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Strikes or insurrections affecting normal supply chains will be recognized.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Total liability is limited to the payments made under the agreement in the six months preceding a claim.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
