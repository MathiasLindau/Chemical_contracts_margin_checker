# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-007)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Kappa Industries**, a New Jersey entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Methanol
- **Base Price:** 700.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 60.0 tons up to a maximum of 300.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Agreement ('Agreement') is made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Kappa Industries ('Buyer'). The Supplier agrees to furnish Methanol as specified in the attached Product Specification Sheet. The relationship will consist of monthly commitments and provides for a flexible delivery schedule as negotiated. The Buyer shall inspect the delivered Product and notify the Supplier of any discrepancies within three days. Should Force Majeure apply, responsibilities shall be suspended for its duration. Disputes under this Agreement shall be governed by applicable New Jersey law, including provisions for mediation prior to litigation.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **10000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Natural obstacles affecting the supply or production.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Seller’s total liability shall not exceed the fees paid for the specific order resulting in the claim.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
