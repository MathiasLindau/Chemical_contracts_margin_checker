# Chemical Supply Agreement CON-2023-0056

Between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (Supplier) and **Siberian Gas Chem** (Buyer). This is a term contract, not a spot confirmation.

## Product specification
The product is Monoethylene Glycol. Monthly nominations shall not be less than 60.0 metric tons and shall not exceed 140.0 metric tons.

## Price and indexation
The firm base price is 480.0 USD per metric ton. An energy adder of 18.0% and a feedstock adder of 0.0% apply to that base. Adders are calculated monthly from the Supplier's published formula.

## Payment
Invoices are due 90 days after the invoice date. Title passes on payment in full; risk passes on delivery.

## Logistics and quality window
Transit shall not exceed 4 days. Remaining shelf life on arrival must be at least 365 days. 7 laytime days are included; thereafter demurrage accrues at the terminal tariff.

## Liquidated damages
Failure to take or supply the minimum monthly quantity is liquidated at **7500.0 USD**, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss.

## Force majeure
Low-water restrictions on the Rhine or Mississippi that cut barge drafts below the booked quantity.

## Law
English law. Exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Arbitration is not required unless both parties later agree in writing.
