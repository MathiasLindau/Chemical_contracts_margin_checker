# International Sale Contract — CON-2023-0063

Seller: CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC. Buyer: **Zurich Fine Organics AG**. Seat of arbitration: Singapore.

## Goods
Styrene Acrylonitrile, commercial grade, unless a tighter spec is attached as Schedule 1.

## Quantity band
Take-or-pay minimum 10.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 50.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

## Commercial terms
Unit price 85.0 EUR/MT. Energy cost recovery 6.0%. Raw-material recovery 16.0%. Settlement currency is the contract currency.

## Credit
Payment within 90 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

## Delivery discipline
Maximum transit 8 days. Minimum remaining life 120 days. Free time 7 days.

## Default money
Liquidated damages of **7500.0 EUR** apply to an uncured volume shortfall.

## Excused non-performance
Sanctions, export-control listings, or a government allocation order covering the product.

## Dispute board
SIAC Rules, one arbitrator, English language. Singapore law governs.
