# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-002)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Beta Chemical Corp.**, a Florida entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Hydrochloric Acid
- **Base Price:** 600.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 30.0 tons up to a maximum of 150.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Agreement made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (herein referred to as 'Seller') and Beta Chemical Corp (herein referred to as 'Buyer') for the purchase of Hydrochloric Acid. The term 'Product' refers to the chemical compound sold hereunder. Quantity, price, and specifications are defined in the attached Schedule A. The Seller warrants that the Product will comply with ACGIH and OSHA standards. Deliveries will remain consistent with the Buyer’s stated needs per Schedule B. Payment shall be made net 60 days post-delivery. Force majeure events as defined in Clause 15 will apply to both parties. Any failure to deliver which is due to Force Majeure shall not be considered a breach of this Agreement. Disputes shall be adjudicated according to the laws of Florida, and each party agrees to waive their rights to a jury trial.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **3000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Includes pandemics, governmental restrictions, or acts of terrorism.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability is capped at the total fees paid under this agreement for the prior six months prior to any claim.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
