# Chemical Supply Agreement CON-2023-0086

Between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (Supplier) and **Bogota Sugar Ethanol SAS** (Buyer). This is a term contract, not a spot confirmation.

## Product specification
The product is Liquid Nitrogen. Monthly nominations shall not be less than 350.0 metric tons and shall not exceed 360.0 metric tons.

## Price and indexation
The firm base price is 310.0 EUR per metric ton. An energy adder of 4.0% and a feedstock adder of 1.0% apply to that base. Adders are calculated monthly from the Supplier's published formula.

## Payment
Invoices are due 14 days after the invoice date. Title passes on payment in full; risk passes on delivery.

## Logistics and quality window
Transit shall not exceed 21 days. Remaining shelf life on arrival must be at least 30 days. 1 laytime days are included; thereafter demurrage accrues at the terminal tariff.

## Liquidated damages
Failure to take or supply the minimum monthly quantity is liquidated at **25000.0 EUR**, which the parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss.

## Force majeure
Low-water restrictions on the Rhine or Mississippi that cut barge drafts below the booked quantity.

## Law
English law. Exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales. Arbitration is not required unless both parties later agree in writing.
