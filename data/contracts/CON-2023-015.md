# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-015)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Sigma Biochemicals**, a Tennessee entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Glycerol
- **Base Price:** 400.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 70.0 tons up to a maximum of 280.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Sales Contract ('Agreement') is between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Seller') and Sigma Biochemicals ('Buyer'). The Seller shall furnish Glycerol conforming to applicable safety standards. Payment shall occur within the standard net terms prescribed in this agreement. Liability shall be confined to the direct losses proven in association with the supplied Product. The parties will handle disputes through non-court resolutions under Tennessee law.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **6000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Any operational emergencies affecting quality assurance processes will stand under force majeure.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The liability limit is set to the equivalent amounts incurred by the Client in the last agreement executed by the parties.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
