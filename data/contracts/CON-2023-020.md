# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-020)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Beta Core Chemicals**, a Arizona entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Potassium Nitrate
- **Base Price:** 410.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 50.0 tons up to a maximum of 300.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Sales Contract ('Contract') is made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Seller') and Beta Core Chemicals ('Buyer') concerning the sale of Potassium Nitrate. The Seller assures quality verification via testing before shipment, while the Buyer undertakes risk acceptance upon delivery. Payment shall be executed in conformity with agreed terms. For any disputes, arbitration will be pursued in the State of Arizona.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **6000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Political conditions resulting in abnormal supply chain operations shall apply.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier’s liability shall depend strictly on the adherence to limits established within this Agreement.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
