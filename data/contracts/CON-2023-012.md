# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-012)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Omicron Materials LLC**, a Washington entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Acetic Acid
- **Base Price:** 490.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 60.0 tons up to a maximum of 250.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Agreement ('Agreement') made effective on the date signed between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Omicron Materials LLC ('Client'). The Supplier shall provide Acetic Acid under the terms set forth within this document. The standards for Product quality are specified in the attached quality assurance plan, and both parties shall adhere to the commitments for quantity and payment terms. The designated delivery terms are FOBC as per Exhibit A. The parties agree to adhere to the laws of Washington State for governance over this Agreement.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **5000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Natural phenomena impacting supply lines qualify as force majeure.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability shall be the lower of the total contract value or the losses incurred due to the breach.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
