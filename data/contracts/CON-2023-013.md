# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-013)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Pi Chemical Corporation**, a Minnesota entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Hydrogen Peroxide
- **Base Price:** 780.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 70.0 tons up to a maximum of 350.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Master Supply Agreement ('Agreement') is made as of this date by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Pi Chemical Corporation ('Buyer'). The Supplier commits to delivering Hydrogen Peroxide in specified quantities, complying with industry standards. Both parties will undertake briefings on quality testing procedures prior to commencement. Delivery terms will activate upon issuance of order confirmation with FOB policies stated in detail. Both parties acknowledge that unforeseen circumstances such as hurricanes may trigger Force Majeure clauses, suspending obligations. Disputes will be resolved under Minnesota law.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **7500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Hurricanes or devastating weather events affecting state-wide production will invoke force majeure.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Any liability related to this Agreement shall be limited to the sum outlined in prior transactions between the parties for the twelve months preceding the event giving rise.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
