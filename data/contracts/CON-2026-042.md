- **Base Price:** 1500.0 USD per metric ton.

- **Raw Material Adder:** 8.0% surcharge on base price for raw material cost adjustments.

## 2. Logistical Constraints & Terms

- **Minimum Shelf Life:** 60 days upon delivery.
- **Demurrage / Free Container Days:** 10 days included.
