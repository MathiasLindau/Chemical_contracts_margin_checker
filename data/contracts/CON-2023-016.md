# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-016)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Tau Industrial Services**, a Kentucky entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Chlorine
- **Base Price:** 670.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 55.0 tons up to a maximum of 300.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Agreement ('Agreement') is executed between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Tau Industrial Services ('Buyer'). The Supplier will provide Chlorine under the defined terms effective immediately. Ensuring Product quality will be paramount and the Buyer reserves rights outlined in the Warranty Clause. The disputes will be handled according to the law of Kentucky, favoring arbitration where feasible to resolve any discrepancies arising therein. The Force Majeure clauses are enforceable under sudden and unexpected events.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **5500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Natural disasters impacting delivery schedules will apply.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Seller's liability shall not exceed the total shipped amounts corresponding to the current contract term.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
