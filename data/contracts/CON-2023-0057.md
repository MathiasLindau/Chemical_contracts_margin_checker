Seller: CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC. Buyer: **Gulf Alkali Trading**. Seat of arbitration: Singapore.

## Goods
Diethylene Glycol, commercial grade, unless a tighter spec is attached as Schedule 1.

Take-or-pay minimum 15.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 35.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

Unit price 1150.0 CHF/MT. Energy cost recovery 6.0%. Raw-material recovery 5.0%. Settlement currency is the contract currency.

Payment within 45 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

Maximum transit 4 days. Minimum remaining life 730 days. Free time 3 days.

## Default money
Liquidated damages of **2500.0 CHF** apply to an uncured volume shortfall.

## Excused non-performance

## Dispute board
