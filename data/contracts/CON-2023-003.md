# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-003)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Gamma Chemicals Inc.**, a California entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Ethylene Glycol
- **Base Price:** 800.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 100.0 tons up to a maximum of 500.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This contract is made by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (the 'Seller') and Gamma Chemicals Inc. (the 'Buyer'). Under this agreement, the Seller shall supply Ethylene Glycol as specified in Attachment A. 'Force Majeure' encompasses events beyond party control, including but not limited to self-inflicted, agent-related failures and external regulations. The Buyer accepts that delivery schedules are estimates. Title and risk of loss of the Product pass to the Buyer upon delivery at the FOB point stated herein. The Buyer may reject non-conforming Product and the Seller shall have the opportunity to remedy the defect within 30 days. In cases of contractual disputes, the parties agree to mediation before seeking formal arbitration in accordance with California law.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **7000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Any interruption due to unforeseen governmental reforms or supplier failures.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Seller’s aggregate liability shall not exceed the total amount paid by the Buyer in the prior twelve months.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
