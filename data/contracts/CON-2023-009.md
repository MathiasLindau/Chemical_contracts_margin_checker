# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-009)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Mu Gamma Chemical Co.**, a Michigan entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Isopropyl Alcohol
- **Base Price:** 420.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 80.0 tons up to a maximum of 350.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Agreement ('Agreement') specifying terms is established by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (the 'Supplier') and Mu Gamma Chemical Co. (the 'Buyer'). The Supplier will provide Isopropyl Alcohol subject to stipulations that ensure quality assurance per regulatory standards. Buyer shall ensure timely payments as per the payment schedule. Any claims regarding quality must be submitted within five days post-delivery. Should delivery be impossible due to Force Majeure, the Supplier will make reasonable efforts to notify the Buyer. This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with Michigan law, with mediation provisions in case of dispute.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **4500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Any disruptions caused by global crises will be evidenced under this contract.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier’s liability hereunder shall not exceed the total amount charged for goods under the contract during the twelve-month period prior to the incident.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
