# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2026-047)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Industrial Coatings LLC**.

## 1. Scope, Pricing & Dynamic Adders
- **Product:** Pigment Dispersions
- **Base Price:** 700.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 60.0 tons up to a maximum of 120.0 tons.
- **Energy Adder:** 2.0% surcharge on base price for energy fluctuations.
- **Raw Material Adder:** 4.0% surcharge on base price for raw material cost adjustments.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Logistical Constraints & Terms
- **Max Transport Duration:** 10 days.
- **Minimum Shelf Life:** 40 days upon delivery.
- **Demurrage / Free Container Days:** 3 days included.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **3500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Suppliers’ choice of terms.

## 5. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
