# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-018)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Omega Solutions**, a New Hampshire entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Sulfate
- **Base Price:** 300.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 30.0 tons up to a maximum of 180.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Agreement ('Agreement') formed here is entered between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Omega Solutions ('Buyer') for Sulfate. The defined terms shall ensure quality assurance compliance to local and state regulations. Payment shall occur under net 60-day conditions, with penalties for breach as specified herein. Parties agree to negotiation before resorting to formal legal proceedings, resolved through arbitration.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **2500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Identify any disruptions due to state-imposed regulations for deliveries under this contract.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
The Supplier's total liability direct connection to this Agreement shall be limited to the maximum amounts paid in the last six months of transactions.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
