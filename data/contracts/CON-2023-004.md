# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-004)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Delta Manufacturing Ltd.**, a New York entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Ammonium Nitrate
- **Base Price:** 300.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 25.0 tons up to a maximum of 100.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Master Purchase Agreement is made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Seller') and Delta Manufacturing Ltd. ('Buyer') for the sale of Ammonium Nitrate. Defined under this contract, 'Product' refers to the chemical compound purchased herein. Buyer agrees to purchase a minimum of 25 tons and a maximum of 100 tons monthly, subject to the terms listed in Section 4. The Seller bears the responsibility of ensuring the Product meets the quality specifications listed in Schedule A. Any breaches of contract or failure to fulfill the delivery obligations may incur penalties delineated in Section 13. It is agreed that both parties will partake in mediation before resorting to arbitration. This Contract shall be evaluated under the laws of New York State.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **2000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Natural disasters and fire resulting in loss of production.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability is limited to the contract total for the order where the breach occurred.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
