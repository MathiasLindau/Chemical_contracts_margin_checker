# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-001)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Acme Industrial Solutions**, a Texas entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Sodium Hypochlorite
- **Base Price:** 450.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 50.0 tons up to a maximum of 200.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Contract governs the sale of Sodium Hypochlorite by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC (hereinafter referred to as 'Seller') to Acme Industrial Solutions (hereinafter referred to as 'Buyer'). The Seller agrees to deliver and the Buyer agrees to purchase the product under the terms specified herein. 'Product' shall mean Sodium Hypochlorite, and the specifications thereof shall meet industry standards. The Seller warrants that the Product will be free from defects in material and workmanship. Quality inspections shall occur at the Seller's facility, upon which the Buyer may conduct reasonable testing to ensure compliance. All deliveries are to be made FOB specifying the delivery location. Risk of loss transfers to the Buyer upon delivery. This Contract shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any disputes arising from this Contract shall be resolved through arbitration. In case of any dispute, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **5000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Force Majeure shall include natural disasters, war, or labor strikes affecting deliveries.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability limited to damages directly incurred, not exceeding the amount paid under this contract.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
