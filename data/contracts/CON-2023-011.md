# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-011)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Xi Chemical Industries**, a Pennsylvania entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Phenol
- **Base Price:** 950.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 40.0 tons up to a maximum of 150.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
The Supply Agreement ('Agreement') made herein is between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Seller') and Xi Chemical Industries ('Buyer'). The Seller shall deliver Phenol under specified conditions in Attachment A. The Buyer accepts to purchase a minimum quantity per term and recognizes all specifications detailed herein must be met. Force Majeure events, when invoked, will relieve the parties of their obligations for the duration of those conditions. All transactions and disputes shall be governed by the laws of Pennsylvania.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **9000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Any acts of war that disrupt trade will be a legitimate excuse for performance failure.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Limit of liability for both parties matches the total cost of breach as explicitly defined in this Agreement.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
