# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-017)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Upsilon Chemical Supply**, a Oregon entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Sodium Phosphate
- **Base Price:** 370.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 45.0 tons up to a maximum of 200.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Supply Contract ('Contract') is made between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') and Upsilon Chemical Supply ('Buyer') for Sodium Phosphate. The Supplier assures compliance with safety standards. The agreements will specify terms of Product specifications within Attachment A. The Supplier will notify the Buyer in case of inability to supply the Product, explaining all Force Majeure claims as well. In case of disputes, this Agreement will be adjudicated under the laws of the State of Oregon.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **5000.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Insufficient supply chain interruptions due to external governmental control qualify as force majeure events.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Limits of liability are focused on direct, measurable financial losses directly linked to the agreement.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
