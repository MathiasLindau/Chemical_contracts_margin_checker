# International Sale Contract — CON-2023-0051

Seller: CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC. Buyer: **Nordic Pulp AB**. Seat of arbitration: Singapore.

## Goods
Methanol, commercial grade, unless a tighter spec is attached as Schedule 1.

## Quantity band
Take-or-pay minimum 80.0 MT/month. Optional upside to 120.0 MT/month subject to Seller's confirmation within two business days.

## Commercial terms
Unit price 2100.0 GBP/MT. Energy cost recovery 8.0%. Raw-material recovery 12.0%. Settlement currency is the contract currency.

## Credit
Payment within 7 days. Seller may require a standby letter of credit if exposure exceeds three months of the minimum volume.

## Delivery discipline
Maximum transit 16 days. Minimum remaining life 60 days. Free time 5 days.

## Default money
Liquidated damages of **40000.0 GBP** apply to an uncured volume shortfall.

## Excused non-performance
Named windstorms, named floods, and canal closures that block the nominated load port for more than 72 hours.

## Dispute board
SIAC Rules, one arbitrator, English language. Singapore law governs.
