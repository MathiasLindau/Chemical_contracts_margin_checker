# MASTER AGREEMENT FOR CHEMICAL SALES (CON-2023-019)

This Master Agreement for Chemical Sales ("Agreement") is entered into by and between CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC and **Alpha Chemical Group**, a Colorado entity.

## 1. Scope and Transaction Procedure
- **Product:** Citric Acid
- **Base Price:** 390.0 USD per metric ton.
- **Volume Commitments:** Minimum monthly volume of 40.0 tons up to a maximum of 220.0 tons.
- **Payment Terms:** Net 60 days from the date of invoice.

## 2. Comprehensive Legal Terms & Conditions
This Agreement ('Contract') is entered into by CarbonFree Chemicals SPE I LLC ('Supplier') with Alpha Chemical Group ('Buyer') for Citric Acid. Under this Contract, it is acknowledged that compliance with quality standards is paramount. The terms specify FOB delivery methods. Breach penalties are clearly outlined in Section 12, allowing for mediation prior to litigation if necessary under Colorado law.

## 3. Breach of Contract and Penalties
In the event of a material breach, short delivery, or failure to meet the agreed minimum monthly volume without a valid justification, the defaulting party shall be subject to a liquidated financial penalty of **3500.0 USD**.

## 4. Force Majeure Clauses
Catastrophic events that impede delivery operations will fall under this clause.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Liability is expressly limited to damages arising out of only direct losses associated with this transaction.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
